Pronunciation Page

Bopomofo (Zhuyin 注音) is a phonetic system used in Taiwan to help learners pronounce Mandarin Chinese accurately. Each symbol represents a specific sound, similar to letters in the English alphabet. Learning Bopomofo helps students build a strong foundation in listening, pronunciation, and reading Chinese.

Mandarin Chinese has four main tones. The meaning of a word can change completely depending on its tone. Mastering the four tones is essential for clear communication and understanding spoken Chinese. In this section, you will learn Bopomofo symbols, their corresponding Pinyin, example words, and correct pronunciation through audio practice.
