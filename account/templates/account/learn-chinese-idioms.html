Chinese Idioms （成語） for Everyday Conversation

36 high-frequency chengyu — Traditional characters, BoPoMoFo, Pinyin, and an example sentence for every one.

Why Idioms Are the Shortcut to Sounding Natural

Chinese speakers pepper everyday speech with chengyu （成語） — compact four-character idioms, most of them centuries old. Saying a friend helped you in a crisis is fine; saying he was 雪中送炭 ("sending charcoal in the snow") is how a native speaker actually says it. A small set of well-chosen idioms makes your Chinese warmer, funnier, and far more expressive.

Body-Part Idioms （身體部位）

Idioms built from body parts — vivid, memorable, and used constantly in everyday speech.

Idiom 成語Meaning & example
手忙腳亂
ㄕㄡˇ ㄇㄤˊ ㄐㄧㄠˇ ㄌㄨㄢˋ
shǒu máng jiǎo luàn		to be in a rush and confused, implies being disorganized or panicking
他第一次做飯，弄得手忙腳亂。
Tā dì yī cì zuòfàn, nòng de shǒu máng jiǎo luàn. — It was his first time cooking, and he made a mess in a panic.
目瞪口呆
ㄇㄨˋ ㄉㄥˋ ㄎㄡˇ ㄉㄞˉ
mù dèng kǒu dāi		eyes staring and mouth frozen, implies being stunned or speechless with shock
看到那個結果，大家都目瞪口呆。
Kàn dào nàge jiéguǒ, dàjiā dōu mù dèng kǒu dāi. — Seeing that result, everyone was stunned speechless.
口是心非
ㄎㄡˇ ㄕˋ ㄒㄧㄣˉ ㄈㄟˉ
kǒu shì xīn fēi		mouth says yes but heart says no, implies being hypocritical or saying one thing but meaning another
他口是心非，你別相信他。
Tā kǒu shì xīn fēi, nǐ bié xiāngxìn tā. — He says one thing but means another, so don't believe him.
心口如一
ㄒㄧㄣˉ ㄎㄡˇ ㄖㄨˊ ㄧˉ
xīn kǒu rú yī		heart and mouth as one, implies being honest and speaking truthfully from the heart
她是心口如一的人，從不說謊。
Tā shì xīn kǒu rú yī de rén, cóng bù shuōhuǎng. — She is an honest person who speaks from the heart and never lies.
頭頭是道
ㄊㄡˊ ㄊㄡˊ ㄕˋ ㄉㄠˋ
tóu tóu shì dào		speaking in an organized and logical way, implies explaining something clearly and convincingly
他分析得頭頭是道。
Tā fēnxī de tóu tóu shì dào. — He analyzed it clearly and logically.
頭暈目眩
ㄊㄡˊ ㄩㄣˉ ㄇㄨˋ ㄒㄩㄢˋ
tóu yūn mù xuàn		head spinning and eyes dizzy, implies feeling overwhelmed or physically unwell
天氣太熱，我頭暈目眩。
Tiānqì tài rè, wǒ tóu yūn mù xuàn. — The weather is too hot, and I feel dizzy and overwhelmed.
耳濡目染
ㄦˇ ㄖㄨˊ ㄇㄨˋ ㄖㄢˇ
ěr rú mù rǎn		ears soaked and eyes dyed, implies being unconsciously influenced by long-term exposure
他從小耳濡目染，也愛上了音樂。
Tā cóngxiǎo ěr rú mù rǎn, yě ài shàng le yīnyuè. — Influenced since childhood by what he saw and heard, he also fell in love with music.
耳目一新
ㄦˇ ㄇㄨˋ ㄧˉ ㄒㄧㄣˉ
ěr mù yī xīn		ears and eyes refreshed, implies experiencing something impressively new and refreshing
這家餐廳的設計讓人耳目一新。
Zhè jiā cāntīng de shèjì ràng rén ěr mù yī xīn. — The design of this restaurant is refreshing and new.
眼高手低
ㄧㄢˇ ㄍㄠˉ ㄕㄡˇ ㄉㄧˉ
yǎn gāo shǒu dī		eyes high but hands low, implies having high ambitions but lacking real ability
他眼高手低，什麼都做不好。
Tā yǎn gāo shǒu dī, shénme dōu zuò bù hǎo. — He aims high but lacks skill, so he can't do anything well.
眉開眼笑
ㄇㄟˊ ㄎㄞˉ ㄧㄢˇ ㄒㄧㄠˋ
méi kāi yǎn xiào		eyebrows open and eyes smiling, implies being extremely happy and delighted
聽到好消息，她眉開眼笑。
Tīng dào hǎo xiāoxi, tā méi kāi yǎn xiào. — Hearing the good news, she was beaming with joy.
手到擒來
ㄕㄡˇ ㄉㄠˋ ㄑㄧㄣˊ ㄌㄞˊ
shǒu dào qín lái		reach out and capture, implies something very easy to accomplish
這件事對他來說手到擒來。
Zhè jiàn shì duì tā lái shuō shǒu dào qín lái. — This matter is very easy for him to accomplish.
手足無措
ㄕㄡˇ ㄗㄨˊ ㄨˊ ㄘㄨㄛˋ
shǒu zú wú cuò		hands and feet have no place, implies being at a loss and not knowing what to do
遇到突發狀況，他手足無措。
Yù dào tūfā zhuàngkuàng, tā shǒu zú wú cuò. — When faced with an emergency, he was at a loss.
肺腑之言
ㄈㄟˋ ㄈㄨˇ ㄓˉ ㄧㄢˊ
fèi fǔ zhī yán		words from the lungs and organs, implies sincere words from the bottom of one's heart
這是我的肺腑之言，請你相信我。
Zhè shì wǒ de fèi fǔ zhī yán, qǐng nǐ xiāngxìn wǒ. — These are my heartfelt words, please believe me.
肝膽相照
ㄍㄢˉ ㄉㄢˇ ㄒㄧㄤˉ ㄓㄠˋ
gān dǎn xiāng zhào		liver and gallbladder illuminating each other, implies being true and loyal friends who trust each other completely
他們是肝膽相照的好朋友。
Tāmen shì gān dǎn xiāng zhào de hǎo péngyǒu. — They are loyal friends who trust each other completely.
骨瘦如柴
ㄍㄨˇ ㄕㄡˋ ㄖㄨˊ ㄔㄞˊ
gǔ shòu rú chái		bones thin as firewood, implies being extremely skinny or emaciated
他生病後骨瘦如柴。
Tā shēngbìng hòu gǔ shòu rú chái. — After getting sick, he became extremely skinny.
粉身碎骨
ㄈㄣˇ ㄕㄣˉ ㄙㄨㄟˋ ㄍㄨˇ
fěn shēn suì gǔ		body powdered and bones shattered, implies being willing to sacrifice everything or facing total destruction
為了家人，他粉身碎骨也在所不惜。
Wèi le jiārén, tā fěn shēn suì gǔ yě zài suǒ bù xī. — For his family, he would sacrifice everything without hesitation.
血口噴人
ㄒㄩㄝˋ ㄎㄡˇ ㄆㄣˉ ㄖㄣˊ
xuè kǒu pēn rén		spitting blood from the mouth at someone, implies making vicious false accusations against someone
你別血口噴人，我根本沒做。
Nǐ bié xuè kǒu pēn rén, wǒ gēnběn méi zuò. — Don't make vicious false accusations, I didn't do it at all.
胸有成竹
ㄒㄩㄥˉ ㄧㄡˇ ㄔㄥˊ ㄓㄨˊ
xiōng yǒu chéng zhú		chest has mature bamboo, implies having a well-thought-out plan and being confident
面對考試，他胸有成竹。
Miàn duì kǎoshì, tā xiōng yǒu chéng zhú. — Facing the exam, he was fully confident and well prepared.

Nature Idioms （大自然）

Weather, seasons, and landscapes — Chinese idioms love borrowing from the natural world.

Idiom 成語Meaning & example
一石二鳥
ㄧˉ ㄕˊ ㄦˋ ㄋㄧㄠˇ
yī shí èr niǎo		To kill two birds with one stone; to achieve two goals with a single action.
我想去買東西，也想看朋友，去台北可以一石二鳥。
wǒ xiǎng qù mǎi dōng xī，yě xiǎng kàn péng yǒu，qù tái běi kě yǐ yī shí èr niǎo。 — I want to go shopping and also want to see a friend, going to Taipei can kill two birds with one stone.
雨過天晴
ㄩˇ ㄍㄨㄛˋ ㄊㄧㄢˉ ㄑㄧㄥˊ
yǔ guò tiān qíng		The rain passes and the sky clears; troubles pass and things return to normal or better.
雨過天晴，我們可以出去玩了。
yǔ guò tiān qíng，wǒ men kě yǐ chū qù wán le。 — The rain has passed and the sky cleared, we can go out and play now.
山明水秀
ㄕㄢˉ ㄇㄧㄥˊ ㄕㄨㄟˇ ㄒㄧㄡˋ
shān míng shuǐ xiù		Bright mountains and elegant waters; describing beautiful natural scenery.
這個地方山明水秀，我很喜歡。
zhè ge dì fāng shān míng shuǐ xiù，wǒ hěn xǐ huān。 — This place has beautiful scenery, I really like it.
鳥語花香
ㄋㄧㄠˇ ㄩˇ ㄏㄨㄚˉ ㄒㄧㄤˉ
niǎo yǔ huā xiāng		Birds sing and flowers give forth fragrance; describing a beautiful spring scene.
春天來了，公園裡鳥語花香。
chūn tiān lái le，gōng yuán lǐ niǎo yǔ huā xiāng。 — Spring has come, the park is full of singing birds and fragrant flowers.
風平浪靜
ㄈㄥˉ ㄆㄧㄥˊ ㄌㄤˋ ㄐㄧㄥˋ
fēng píng làng jìng		The wind is calm and the waves are still; describing a peaceful situation or calm waters.
今天海上風平浪靜，我們可以去釣魚。
jīn tiān hǎi shàng fēng píng làng jìng，wǒ men kě yǐ qù diào yú。 — Today the sea is calm, we can go fishing.
水到渠成
ㄕㄨㄟˇ ㄉㄠˋ ㄑㄩˊ ㄔㄥˊ
shuǐ dào qú chéng		When water arrives a channel is formed; meaning things succeed naturally when conditions are ripe.
你努力學習，成功就會水到渠成。
nǐ nǔ lì xué xí，chéng gōng jiù huì shuǐ dào qú chéng。 — If you study hard, success will come naturally when conditions are right.
春暖花開
ㄔㄨㄣˉ ㄋㄨㄢˇ ㄏㄨㄚˉ ㄎㄞˉ
chūn nuǎn huā kāi		Spring is warm and flowers bloom; describing a pleasant spring scene or a good turn of events.
春暖花開，我們去看花吧。
chūn nuǎn huā kāi，wǒ men qù kàn huā ba。 — Spring is warm and flowers are blooming, let us go see the flowers.
秋高氣爽
ㄑㄧㄡˉ ㄍㄠˉ ㄑㄧˋ ㄕㄨㄤˇ
qiū gāo qì shuǎng		Autumn is high and the air is crisp; describing clear and pleasant autumn weather.
秋高氣爽，我想去爬山。
qiū gāo qì shuǎng，wǒ xiǎng qù pá shān。 — The autumn air is crisp, I want to go hiking.
雪中送炭
ㄒㄩㄝˇ ㄓㄨㄥˉ ㄙㄨㄥˋ ㄊㄢˋ
xuě zhōng sòng tàn		To send charcoal in the snow; meaning to provide help when someone is in urgent need.
他在我最難的時候幫我，真是雪中送炭。
tā zài wǒ zuì nán de shí hòu bāng wǒ，zhēn shì xuě zhōng sòng tàn。 — He helped me when I was in the most difficulty, truly sending charcoal in the snow.
海闊天空
ㄏㄞˇ ㄎㄨㄛˋ ㄊㄧㄢˉ ㄎㄨㄥˉ
hǎi kuò tiān kōng		The sea is broad and the sky is vast; describing openness, freedom, or boundless imagination.
我們一起聊天，海闊天空，很開心。
wǒ men yī qǐ liáo tiān，hǎi kuò tiān kōng，hěn kāi xīn。 — We chatted together freely and happily.

Friendship Idioms （友誼）

How Chinese describes loyalty, trust, and bonds between friends — some of the warmest idioms in the language.

Idiom 成語Meaning & example
一見如故
ㄧˉ ㄐㄧㄢˋ ㄖㄨˊ ㄍㄨˋ
yī jiàn rú gù		To feel like old friends at first meeting; implying instant chemistry and deep connection with someone new.
我和他一見如故，聊得很開心。
wǒ hé tā yī jiàn rú gù，liáo de hěn kāi xīn。 — He and I felt like old friends at first meeting, we chatted very happily.
情同手足
ㄑㄧㄥˊ ㄊㄨㄥˊ ㄕㄡˇ ㄗㄨˊ
qíng tóng shǒu zú		Feelings like hands and feet; implying a bond as close as siblings.
我和我的好朋友情同手足。
wǒ hé wǒ de hǎo péng yǒu qíng tóng shǒu zú。 — My good friend and I are as close as siblings.
推心置腹
ㄊㄨㄟˉ ㄒㄧㄣˉ ㄓˋ ㄈㄨˋ
tuī xīn zhì fù		To place one's heart into another's belly; implying to confide in someone openly and sincerely.
他對我推心置腹，我很感動。
tā duì wǒ tuī xīn zhì fù，wǒ hěn gǎn dòng。 — He confided in me openly and sincerely, I was very touched.
風雨同舟
ㄈㄥˉ ㄩˇ ㄊㄨㄥˊ ㄓㄡˉ
fēng yǔ tóng zhōu		To share a boat in wind and rain; implying to stand together through hardships.
我們是好朋友，要風雨同舟。
wǒ men shì hǎo péng yǒu，yào fēng yǔ tóng zhōu。 — We are good friends, we must stand together through hardships.
志同道合
ㄓˋ ㄊㄨㄥˊ ㄉㄠˋ ㄏㄜˊ
zhì tóng dào hé		Same ambition and same path; implying people who share the same goals and interests.
我們志同道合，所以常常一起工作。
wǒ men zhì tóng dào hé，suǒ yǐ cháng cháng yī qǐ gōng zuò。 — We share the same goals and interests, so we often work together.
心心相印
ㄒㄧㄣˉ ㄒㄧㄣˉ ㄒㄧㄤˉ ㄧㄣˋ
xīn xīn xiāng yìn		Hearts mutually imprinted; implying two people understand each other deeply without words.
他和他的太太心心相印。
tā hé tā de tài tai xīn xīn xiāng yìn。 — He and his wife understand each other deeply.
患難之交
ㄏㄨㄢˋ ㄋㄢˋ ㄓˉ ㄐㄧㄠˉ
huàn nàn zhī jiāo		Friendship formed through adversity; implying a bond that is tested and proven in hard times.
他是我的患難之交，我永遠不會忘記他。
tā shì wǒ de huàn nàn zhī jiāo，wǒ yǒng yuǎn bù huì wàng jì tā。 — He is my friend tested through adversity, I will never forget him.
一視同仁
ㄧˉ ㄕˋ ㄊㄨㄥˊ ㄖㄣˊ
yī shì tóng rén		To treat all people equally; implying fairness and no favoritism toward anyone.
老師對每個學生都一視同仁。
lǎo shī duì měi ge xué shēng dōu yī shì tóng rén。 — The teacher treats every student equally.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Chinese idiom (chengyu)?

A chengyu (成語) is a fixed four-character expression, usually drawn from a classical story or image, that carries a meaning bigger than its literal words. Native speakers use them constantly in conversation, writing, and media — learning the common ones unlocks a more native-sounding, expressive way of speaking Chinese.

How many Chinese idioms do I need to learn?

There are thousands of chengyu, but a working set of a few dozen everyday idioms covers the ones you will actually hear in conversation, TV dramas, and news. The 36 idioms on this page are a practical starter set chosen for daily usefulness.

Are these idioms in Traditional or Simplified Chinese?

Traditional. Every idiom on this page is shown in Traditional characters with both BoPoMoFo (Zhuyin) and Pinyin, the way PandaSpeak teaches all of its material.

What is the best way to memorize Chinese idioms?

Learn each idiom with its story image and one real example sentence, then use it out loud the same day. Grouping idioms by theme — body parts, nature, friendship — gives your memory hooks to hang them on, which is why this page and the free PDF are organized that way.
