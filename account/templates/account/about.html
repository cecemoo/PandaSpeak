About PandaSpeak

Independent online Traditional Chinese learning for adults worldwide.

Who We Are

PandaSpeak is an independent online Traditional Chinese learning platform designed primarily for adult learners. PandaSpeak is owned by Orange Jump Jump and is based in Houston, Texas, United States.

Houston is PandaSpeak's administrative base, not a geographic limit on participation. Students and teachers may join PandaSpeak from anywhere in the world.

Learn Anywhere, Anytime

PandaSpeak's online learning materials are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Learners can use vocabulary, practical sentences, Chinese expressions, pronunciation resources, BoPoMoFo and Pinyin support, listening activities, and other learning tools according to their own schedules.

🏮 🐼

Learn the Culture Behind the Language

Available for Level II & Level III students

Chinese is more than vocabulary, sentences, and expressions. As students explore higher learning levels, PandaSpeak Cultural Insights introduce the traditions, everyday customs, etiquette, food, communication styles, regional differences, history, and modern life behind the Chinese they are learning.

🌱 Level II — Everyday Culture

Explore practical cultural knowledge for everyday situations, including dining etiquette, tea culture, gift giving, family relationships, lucky numbers, and traditional customs.

🌏 Level III — Deeper Cultural Understanding

Go deeper into regional differences, history, traditional festivals in modern life, workplace culture, communication styles, and contemporary Chinese-speaking societies.

Level I students can see Cultural Insights on their dashboard as a future learning feature. Cultural Insights become available when Level II or Level III is selected.

Worldwide Online Tutoring

Private and group tutoring sessions are conducted online. Teachers and students can participate from different countries and time zones. PandaSpeak's tutoring booking system displays session times according to the relevant time zone for the teacher and student, helping participants coordinate sessions regardless of where they live.

Our Focus

PandaSpeak focuses on practical Traditional Chinese speaking and listening for real-life communication. Learning resources use BoPoMoFo alongside Pinyin pronunciation support to make Chinese pronunciation more accessible to adult learners.

Official PandaSpeak Website

The official PandaSpeak website is pandaspeak.org. For questions about PandaSpeak, please use the Contact page or email pandaspeaksupport@gmail.com.
